- (decision) IDLE Band model (Reid, replaces N_idle floor + Vpp threshold). During idle calibration the app tracks a running MIN and MAX of the raw bio-signal (the double on the BehaviorSubject stream); each sample only widens the band. Result IDLE_BAND = [running_min, running_max], e.g. samples 100/200/50/240/35/60 -> [35, 240]. NO margin/hysteresis -- band is the raw min/max. During monitoring: a VALID BREATH requires inhale to drive the signal >= upper_bound AND exhale to drive it <= lower_bound (inhale = high signal, exhale = low signal). Any phase that stays inside the band (e.g. inhale 230 < 240, or exhale 40 > 35) is a 'stop breathing' signal. Open: idle window duration (currently 5-10s, UX pins 10s), sensor worn vs bedside during idle sample, apnea-event persistence threshold, hypopnea survival, wear guardrail, whether volumetric L/s survives as a display unit.
- (decision) Apnea event threshold: KEEP the AASM medical standard (Reid: 'stick with medical standard'). In the IDLE Band model this restates as: no valid band excursion (signal stays inside [lower_bound, upper_bound], i.e. no inhale >= upper AND exhale <= lower cycle) for >= 10 s continuously -> one apnea event, feeds AHI. The AASM 'complete cessation / >=90% airflow reduction >=10s' wording is REWORDED to 'no valid band excursion >=10s' but the 10s threshold and the AHI severity scale (<5 / 5-15 / 15-30 / >=30) are unchanged. Tier-1 alarm continues to follow the existing NFR-6.2 IEC 60601-1-8 escalation (medium 10-20s, high >20s) -- no separate custom alarm-onset threshold.
- (decision) Hypopnea DROPPED from MVP1 (Reid). D-BAND is airflow-only -- no SpO2, no EEG -- so it cannot score hypopnea to the AASM standard (needs >=3% desat or arousal). MVP1 detects APNEA only. Consequence/ripple: the nightly metric becomes an Apnea Index (AI), not AHI -- 'AHI' appears throughout PRD, UX (HomeSummaryCardOrganism, MOB_SLEEP_SUMMARY, WeeklyTrendCardOrganism, DESIGN.md), architecture (SessionSummary.ahi_score), research, and epics.md and all need the AHI->AI rename or an explicit 'apnea-only index' caveat. NFR-6.1 loses the hypopnea clause. Open: whether the <5/5-15/15-30/>=30 severity scale (an AHI scale) is kept relabeled+caveated for AI, or severity bands are dropped.
