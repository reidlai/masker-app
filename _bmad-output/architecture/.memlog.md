- (decision) AD-12 (App-Boot Unified Reactive Bio-Signal Ingestion Queue). Binds: app bootstrap/main(), BLE background receiver service, every IBLESensorDriver impl, and all downstream consumers (BleBloc, ApneaEvaluator, MeasurementPage, Stage-1/Stage-2 calibration, SleepMonitoringBloc). Prevents: per-screen/per-phase BLE subscriptions each opening their own GATT channel; divergent queue primitives (plain StreamController/PublishSubject) that drop latest-value replay for late subscribers; calibration vs monitoring racing to own the connection lifecycle. Rule: on launch the receiver service starts (Android foreground service connectedDevice/dataSync type; iOS bluetooth-central UIBackgroundModes) and binds exactly one active IBLESensorDriver by DI; every inbound sample (physical GATT notification OR BleTelemetryService simulator tick) is pushed via .add() into a single process-wide BehaviorSubject<double> exposed as thermalStream/signalStream; all consumers subscribe to that one ValueStream and none open their own BLE subscription or a second queue; queue identity is stable across a driver swap.
- (assumption) Boot starts the receiver SERVICE + unified queue + DI-bound driver; the physical BLE radio connection (scanAndConnect) stays lazy — established when a bound D-BAND is in range or the first consumer needs it, then held alive by the foreground service for the session. Rationale: preserves AD-06 <8% / 8h battery budget and avoids boot-time scans with no device bound. Simulator driver (in-process) streams from boot. FLAG for user confirmation: alternative reading of FR-1.11 is a truly always-on radio link from launch.
